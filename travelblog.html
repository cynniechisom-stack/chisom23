:✈️: My Trip to Enugu

Posted on Oct 12, 2026 by chisom

Last holiday I visited Enugu, the Coal City. It was my first time travelling by road for 6 hours!

Places I visited

  • Ngwo Pine Forest
  • Miliken Hill
  • Awhum Waterfall

My best experience

The waterfall was amazing. The water was very cold and the rocks were tall. I took many pictures.

Tip: If you visit Enugu, go with comfortable shoes.
