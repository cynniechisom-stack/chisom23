:stew: Nigerian Jollof Rice Recipe

The most popular party food in Nigeria!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups Rice
  • 5 Tomatoes
  • 2 Onions
  • Pepper, Curry, Thyme
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken / Fish

Steps

  1. Blend tomatoes and pepper
  2. Fry in oil with onions
  3. Add water and seasoning
  4. Add washed rice
  5. Cook on low heat until done
  6. Serve with chicken

Cooking time: 1 hour | Serves: 5 people
